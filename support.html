Using OneThing

How does OneThing choose my one thing?

In this order: a task you picked yourself, then tasks marked for Today, then tasks with a date in the next two days, then tasks marked Important, then your inbox order (drag to reorder). Tasks you skipped wait 12 hours, and resting tasks (snoozed or Someday) aren't suggested until they're ready. Tap the label above the task to see why it was chosen. You can turn suggestions off in Settings › Choosing.

How do I pick a different task?

Tap Not now › Choose something else, or open the Inbox, touch and hold a task, and choose “Make it my one thing”.

What does “Make it smaller” do?

It suggests a tiny first step for the task, from ideas built into the app, on your device. Pick one or write your own. It becomes a step of the task and shows on Now until you finish it. Your original task stays as it is.

How do timers work?

Tap Start for a session without a timer, or touch and hold Start to pick 5, 10, 15 or 25 minutes. You can also set a default in Settings › Focus. The time is kept from timestamps, so it stays right if you leave the app or restart your iPhone. Finishing a session asks whether the task is done; it never marks it done on its own unless you turn that on.

How do repeating tasks work?

A repeating task is one task that comes back. When you finish it, it rests until the next day it repeats. Missed days don't pile up as duplicates.

Why don't I see a reminder?

OneThing asks to send notifications only when you turn on a reminder or start a timed session. If you said no, turn them on in Settings › Notifications › OneThing.

Widgets, Live Activities and Siri

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, and search for OneThing. “Your One Thing” shows the current task or focus timer; “Add a Task” opens quick capture.

The timer on my Lock Screen shows “--”.

On a locked iPhone, iOS hides the seconds of timers until you unlock. The session keeps running normally. You can turn the Lock Screen timer off in Settings › Focus › Show on Lock Screen.

What can I ask Siri?

“Add a task to OneThing”, “What's my one thing in OneThing”, “Start focusing in OneThing”, “Mark my one thing done in OneThing” and “Skip my task in OneThing”. All of them also appear in the Shortcuts app.

Why do widgets and Siri say “Your next thing” instead of my task?

When “Hide task names” or the app lock is on, OneThing keeps task text off widgets, the Lock Screen and Siri's answers.

iCloud and your data

How do I sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices and keep Settings › iCloud › Sync with iCloud turned on in OneThing. Changes made offline sync when you're back online. Turning sync on or off applies the next time you open the app.

Sync isn't working.

Check that you're signed in to iCloud, that OneThing is allowed under Settings › your name › iCloud, and that you have iCloud storage left. OneThing's Settings › iCloud shows the current status. Your tasks always stay on your device, so nothing is lost while sync waits.

Is iCloud sync a backup?

No. Sync mirrors changes, including deletions, to your other devices. To keep your own copy, use Settings › Your data › Export as JSON now and then. You can import that file again later.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Your data has options to delete completed tasks, reset preferences, or delete all data. With sync on, deleting all data removes it from your other devices too. With sync off, you can separately delete OneThing's data from iCloud. Deleting the app removes its data from that device.
